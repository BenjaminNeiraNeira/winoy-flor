Wiñoy Flor Card Battle

How to play

  1. 1

    You and the computer each get 5 cards from the festival: characters, symbols, instruments, food and drinks.

  2. 2

    Each round, play one card and choose one of its three powers: Bloom, Fire or Harmony.

  3. 3

    The higher number wins the round. Win more rounds out of 5 to win the battle, and learn a fact about the festival in every round.
