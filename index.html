The Festival of the New Sun and the Flowers

Wiñoy Flor is a fictional ten-day festival that celebrates the renewal of nature. It joins the Silleteros Parade of the Feria de las Flores in Medellín with the We Tripantu ceremony, the Mapuche New Year. Both countries celebrate at the same time and connect live at the most important moments.

Wiñoy means “return” in Mapudungun, and Flor is the symbol of Medellín. The festival fuses two symbols: the silleta, the flower disc carried by the silleteros, and the kultrún, the sacred Mapuche drum.

One festival, two countries

🇨🇴 Medellín, Colombia

Venues: Santa Elena, Botanical Garden and Parque de los Deseos.

Weather: mild, about 18–26 °C, with occasional rain.

🇨🇱 Temuco, Chile

Venues: Cerro Ñielol and Plaza Teodoro Schmidt.

Weather: winter, about 3–12 °C, cold and rainy.
